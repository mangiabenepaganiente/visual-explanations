The greenhouse effect isn’t a glass box

A garden greenhouse mostly works as a convection lid — it stops warm air from leaving. Earth’s atmosphere is not a lid. It is largely transparent to visible sunlight and absorbs / re-emits thermal infrared in both directions. The ozone hole is a UV story, not the heat story.

Cause 01

Visible in, infrared out

Sunlight is mostly visible and near-IR. Air is largely transparent to that. The ground warms and radiates thermal infrared — a different band. The atmosphere is not a glass wall to sunlight.

Cause 02

Re-emission, both ways

A gray IR layer of emissivity ε absorbs a fraction of the upwelling heat and radiates the same amount up and down. The downward stream is extra energy the surface must balance — so Ts rises above Teq.

Cause 03

The lid is the garden case

Wood (1909) warmed a box with rock-salt windows that pass thermal IR. It still warmed. A garden greenhouse is mostly a convection lid. Earth’s air is free to rise.

Myth, busted

Ozone does not “let heat in”

The ozone hole is missing stratospheric O₃ over Antarctica in spring. That is a UV health story. Ozone is a minor greenhouse gas; less of it is not the main warming mechanism. Toggle the chip — Ts does not move.

Model, constants, caveats

  1. One published toy: the single-layer gray atmosphere. Pierrehumbert, R. T. (2011). “Infrared radiation and planetary temperature.” Physics Today 64(1), 33–38. Same layer in Pierrehumbert, Principles of Planetary Climate (Cambridge, 2010), Ch. 4. The layer is transparent to solar, gray with emissivity/absorptivity ε in thermal IR, and radiates εσTa⁴ both up and down. Surface and layer are each in energy balance. Result: σ Ts⁴ = S / (1 − ε/2), with S = S0(1−A)/4. At ε = 1, Ts = 2^{1/4} Teq ≈ 303 K. This page uses that equation and no other.
  2. Constants. S0 = 1361 W/m² (Kopp & Lean 2011; IAU 2015 solar constant). σ = 5.670374419×10⁻⁸ W m⁻² K⁻⁴ (CODATA 2018). Default Bond albedo A = 0.30 (classroom value; measured ~0.29). Absorbed S = 1361/4 × 0.70 = 238.175 W/m². Bare-rock Teq = (S/σ)^{1/4} ≈ 254.6 K, cited as ≈ 255 K. Observed global-mean surface air ≈ 288 K.
  3. Fluxes in the cartoon. Incoming at TOA S0/4. Reflected A·S0/4. Surface upwelling σ Ts⁴. Window to space (1−ε)σ Ts⁴. Layer up = layer down = ε σ Ta⁴ = (ε/2) σ Ts⁴ because atmosphere balance forces Ta⁴ = Ts⁴ / 2. Outgoing longwave OLR = (1−ε/2)σ Ts⁴ = S at equilibrium. All solar is absorbed at the surface in this toy (the layer is solar-transparent).
  4. This is not a GCM. One isothermal IR layer. No spectral windows, no water-vapor feedback, no lapse rate, no convection in the energy budget, no clouds as a separate field (albedo is a slider), no geography. Real Earth is a leaking, multi-layer, spectrally selective atmosphere with convection that keeps the tropopause near the radiating level. The 288 K chip only means “pick ε so this toy hits 288 K,” not that ε is a measured optical depth.
  5. Garden greenhouse ≠ atmosphere. R. W. Wood, 1909, “Note on the Theory of the Greenhouse,” Philosophical Magazine 17, 319–320: a rock-salt lid (IR-transparent) still warmed. The dominant garden mechanism is suppressed convection, plus some IR opacity of glass. Fourier (1824), Tyndall (1861), and Arrhenius (1896) already treated the atmosphere as a selective absorber, not a closed box.
  6. Ozone hole ≠ “heat in.” Antarctic spring ozone depletion is a UV-B story (Montreal Protocol). Ozone is a greenhouse gas, so removing it is a small radiative perturbation — not the mechanism of recent surface warming, and not a hole that “lets the Sun’s heat in.” The myth chip draws UV packets; it does not change Ts, Teq, or the IR fluxes.
  7. Drawings are cartoons. Photon packets are countable dots, not a Monte Carlo radiative transfer. Packet rates track the live fluxes only schematically. The glass box is a side-view classroom prop. Molecule count scales with ε. Arrow widths on the budget panel scale with W/m².
